// FAQ

Questions, answered.

What's your turnaround time?
Most short-form edits are delivered within 24–48 hours. Long-form videos typically take 3–5 business days depending on length and complexity. Rush delivery is available on request.
Do you work with raw footage only?
Yes — I work with whatever you provide: raw files, cloud uploads, Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer. If you have brand assets (logos, fonts, colors), send those too.
Can you handle full social media management?
Absolutely. I offer end-to-end packages: content planning, editing, captions, hashtags, scheduling, community management, and monthly performance reports.
What software do you use?
Primarily Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, and DaVinci Resolve for color. Everything is delivered in platform-ready formats (16:9, 9:16, 1:1).
How do we start?
Send me a quick message on WhatsApp or email at noxuzair@gmail.com with your goals and a sample of your content. I'll reply with a plan, timeline, and pricing — usually within a few hours.
